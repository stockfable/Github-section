FAQ

What is CREOVERSE AI Studio?
CREOVERSE AI Studio is a collection of AI-powered editing tools that you can use before licensing and downloading content. It lets you search our catalog of premium content and then edit and download professional-quality images and videos in one place.
Do I have to use CREOVERSE AI Studio?
No, it's completely optional. You can browse, license, and download content without ever using the AI Studio tools.
Do I need a CREOVERSE subscription?
A free account lets you browse and preview. A subscription unlocks unlimited downloads, AI Studio features, and commercial licensing.
How do I access CREOVERSE AI Studio?
Sign in to your CREOVERSE account and click the "AI Studio" button on any image or video detail page.
Can I edit content before I license it?
Yes. You can preview and edit any content in AI Studio before purchasing a license.
Can I upload my own images or video to edit?
Yes, premium subscribers can upload their own media to edit using AI Studio tools.
